# Product: Séance Sheets

Séance Sheets answers plain-English questions about spreadsheets. Upload a CSV or Excel file, ask "What were total sales by month?" or "Which product sold best?", and get a table plus a chart.

## Audience

Small-business owners, analysts, and anyone with a spreadsheet who does not want to learn formulas or pivot tables. For the hackathon, the immediate audience is the judging panel, who must be able to open the app and test it with zero setup.

## Core promise

Ask a question, get a true answer. The query engine is deterministic: it parses the question, runs the computation, and shows what it understood. It never guesses, never invents a number, and says plainly when it cannot understand a question.

## Tone

Spooky but polished, not silly. The séance theme is a wrapper around a genuinely useful tool: "Summon answers from your spreadsheet." Dark theme, purple and green ghost accents, clean typography, readable results.

## What this is not

- Not an AI chatbot. There is no LLM at runtime and no API key.
- Not a spreadsheet editor. It reads data and answers questions; it does not modify files.
- Not a dashboard builder. One question, one answer, done well.
